# Atlas Research Evidence Registry

The files in this folder make paper-dependent Atlas copy auditable while keeping
the standalone app portable.

- sources.mjs and method-sources.mjs contain normalized public metadata,
  page-specific annotations, DOI or stable public links, and development-only
  KnowledgeVault citation keys and BibTeX paths.
- claims.mjs stores every overview and method page's primary-v1 atomic claims.
  A claim records the exact public sentence, evidence verdict, concrete locator,
  evidence paraphrase, reviewed primary artifact and version, plus a review hash.
- sections.mjs and page-sections.mjs map every reviewed section on the overview
  and eight method pages to exact content paths or selectors,
  inline-versus-audit-only display, claim IDs where applicable, and a review
  hash.
- coverage.mjs and page-coverage.mjs record the declared selected-guide scope,
  literature cutoff, two verification passes, categories, exclusions, candidate
  decisions, and completeness status for every page.
- index.mjs renders a public source link immediately after each verified
  paper-dependent claim and builds one-paper-per-entry public bibliographies.
- atlas-source-audit.md is generated by the all-page checker and must remain
  current.

Run the Atlas publication gate from the repository root:

    node webillustration/apps/atlas/source/check_atlas_sources.mjs

Print the hashes of the exact claim/evidence records after an intentional review:

    node webillustration/apps/atlas/source/check_atlas_sources.mjs --print-claim-hashes

Regenerate the audit report after an intentional review:

    node webillustration/apps/atlas/source/check_atlas_sources.mjs --write-report

The vault is the fast resolution layer, not the final evidence layer. Use its
paper note and individual BibTeX record to identify the paper, version, DOI, and
raw artifact. Verify substantive support in the raw primary paper or a stable
public copy. Public pages expose only DOI or public links; vault and raw paths
never render.

Changing registered claim or section text without updating its reviewed hash
makes the checker fail. Every paper-dependent sentence must appear verbatim in
a primary-v1 claim and be supported by a primary artifact; every listed paper
must support at least one such claim. Atlas-authored lab and reader guidance
carries no paper claim and renders no citation. The checker also validates
public bibliography order, source metadata, vault citation-key and DOI
agreement, declared completeness passes, content paths, inline placement, and
report freshness.

As of 19 July 2026, all nine Atlas pages implement the primary atomic-claim
contract. The publication gate covers 126 reviewed sections, 110 atomic claims,
and 71 unique public sources. Each page is a selected guide with an explicit
scope and candidate-decision record, not a claim of systematic-review coverage.
